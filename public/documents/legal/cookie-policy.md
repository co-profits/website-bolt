# Cookie Policy — Company of Profits

**Last updated:** September 15, 2026

## 1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile phone) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work properly, work more efficiently, and provide information to website owners.

This website, operated under the brand **Company of Profits**, uses cookies and similar technologies (such as pixels and local storage) as described in this policy.

## 2. Types of Cookies We Use

**a. Strictly necessary cookies**
Essential for the basic functioning of the Website: they enable navigation, security, load balancing, and core features such as remembering your selected language (English/Spanish) or completing the Prophetic Business Blueprint application form. These do not require prior consent and cannot be disabled through our settings.

**b. Performance and analytics cookies**
These help us understand how visitors interact with the Website (pages visited, time on site, traffic source, device used), in aggregate and, where possible, anonymized form. We use this information to improve the Website's content, structure, and experience.

**c. Functionality cookies**
These remember your preferences (such as language or region) to provide a more personalized experience on future visits.

**d. Form and business-management cookies**
When you complete the Prophetic Business Blueprint application form, our customer relationship management (CRM) platform may use cookies to track conversion and manage the communication associated with your application.

Company of Profits **does not currently use third-party advertising or ad-retargeting cookies**. If this changes in the future, we will update this policy and, where required by law, obtain your specific consent before activating them.

## 3. Third-Party Cookies

Some of our technology providers may set their own cookies when you visit the Website, including:

- **Website hosting/content delivery infrastructure** for the public-facing frontend.
- **Web analytics tools**, used to measure aggregate site usage.
- **A customer relationship management (CRM) platform**, used to process Prophetic Business Blueprint applications and related business communications.

These third parties operate under their own privacy and cookie policies, which we encourage you to review independently.

## 4. Legal Basis and Consent

When you first visit the Website, you will be presented with a cookie banner or notice where you can accept, reject, or configure your preferences for non-essential cookies. Strictly necessary cookies are enabled automatically, as they are required for the Website to function.

For users in the United States, where applicable state law provides an opt-out right regarding the sale or sharing of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, please note that Company of Profits does not currently sell or share personal information for such purposes.

## 5. Managing or Deleting Cookies

You can configure your browser to:

- Notify you when a cookie is received.
- Block all cookies or only third-party cookies.
- Delete cookies already stored on your device.

The process varies by browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge, and others); instructions are generally available in each browser's "Help" or "Privacy Settings" menu.

**Please note:** disabling strictly necessary cookies may affect the Website's functionality, including your ability to complete the application form.
