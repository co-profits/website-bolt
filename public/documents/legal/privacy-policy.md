# Privacy Policy — Company of Profits

**Last updated:** September 22, 2026

## Introduction

Company of Profits ("**Company of Profits**," "we," "us," or "our") is a strategic business consulting and prophetic guidance firm for Christian founders. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, protect, and, where applicable, share the personal information of individuals who visit our website, apply for the **Prophetic Business Blueprint**, enroll in one of our programs, or otherwise interact with us (collectively, the "Services").

This policy applies to visitors and users located in the United States, Latin America, and any other jurisdiction from which our website is accessed.

By using our website or submitting the Prophetic Business Blueprint application form, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the website or provide us with your personal information.

## 1. Who Is Responsible for Your Data

**Company of Profits** is the brand responsible for the personal information collected through this website and associated brand channels.

**Single point of contact:** [info@companyofprofits.com](mailto:info@companyofprofits.com)

Any request, inquiry, complaint, or exercise of privacy rights relating to the handling of personal information should be directed to this email address, which is the official and sole contact channel for Company of Profits for purposes of this policy.

## 2. Definitions

- **Personal information/personal data:** any information that identifies, relates to, describes, or could reasonably be linked, directly or indirectly, with a particular individual.
- **Sensitive personal information:** data that touches on private matters such as religious beliefs, where improper use could result in discrimination.
- **Processing:** any operation performed on personal information, such as collection, storage, use, disclosure, or deletion.
- **Service provider/processor:** a third party that processes personal information on our behalf (for example, technology infrastructure or CRM providers).
- **Consent:** your prior, informed, and voluntary agreement to the processing of your personal information.

## 3. Personal Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

**a. Identification and contact information**
Full name, email address, phone number, country and city, job title, and company name.

**b. Business-related information**
Company size (number of employees), business stage, profitability context, degree of founder dependence in daily operations, key challenges, business goals, and other information you voluntarily provide in the Prophetic Business Blueprint application form.

**c. Information related to your faith and spiritual alignment**
Consistent with our explicit Christian identity, our application form may ask about your Christian faith and your willingness to integrate prophetic guidance with business strategy. This information is collected only with your express consent, in the context of a service you voluntarily request, and is handled with heightened confidentiality.

**d. Technical and browsing information**
IP address, device and browser type, pages visited, time on site, referral source, and information collected through cookies and similar technologies (see our [Cookie Policy](/cookie-policy)).

**e. Communications**
The content of emails, messages, or forms you voluntarily send us.

Company of Profits does **not** directly collect sensitive financial data such as credit card or bank account numbers; any payment processing, where applicable, is handled by third-party payment providers subject to their own security standards.

## 4. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information for the following purposes:

1. To evaluate your Prophetic Business Blueprint application and determine whether your business is a suitable fit for our services.
2. To deliver, manage, and follow up on the strategic consulting and prophetic guidance services you engage.
3. To communicate with you about your application, your participation in a program, or any inquiry you submit.
4. To manage our business relationship, including administrative, billing (where applicable), and contractual matters.
5. To send communications about our services, content, or updates, where you have opted in (a secondary purpose you may withdraw at any time).
6. To improve our website, content, and internal processes through aggregated and statistical usage analysis.
7. To comply with applicable legal, accounting, or regulatory obligations.
8. To prevent fraud, protect the security of the website, and enforce our Terms and Conditions.

We do not use your personal information for purposes other than those described here without obtaining your additional consent.

## 5. The Nature of Prophetic Guidance and Sensitive Information

Company of Profits integrates prophetic discernment — understood as guidance drawn from the Holy Spirit — with strategic business consulting. Religious or spiritual information you share during the application process, the Prophetic Business Blueprint, or any program is treated as sensitive personal information, collected only with your express consent, used solely to deliver the service you voluntarily requested, and handled with reinforced confidentiality safeguards.

You may choose not to engage with the website if you prefer not to provide this type of information; however, because prophetic guidance is a mandatory, integrated component of our core services (see our Terms and Conditions), declining to provide this consent may prevent us from delivering the service.

## 6. Legal Bases and Consent

We process your personal information based on your consent, the necessity of processing to perform a contract or service you requested, our legitimate business interests (such as improving our website and services), and compliance with legal obligations. Where a purpose is separate from delivering the core service you requested — such as marketing communications — we will seek your specific, opt-in consent, which you may withdraw at any time.

## 7. Children's Privacy

Our Services are directed exclusively at adult founders, executives, or authorized representatives of established businesses. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18 (or under 13, where the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act, COPPA, applies). If we learn that we have inadvertently collected a child's personal information without appropriate parental consent, we will delete it.

## 8. Service Providers and Third Parties

To operate the website and deliver our services, Company of Profits works with technology providers that act as processors on our behalf, including:

- **Website hosting/content delivery infrastructure** for the public-facing site.
- **A content management system** used as our editorial backend.
- **A customer relationship management (CRM) platform**, used to manage and follow up on applications and related business communications.
- **Web analytics tools**, used to understand aggregate site usage.

These providers process personal information only under our instructions, for the purposes described here, and are subject to confidentiality and security obligations. Company of Profits does not sell your personal information, and has not ever sold personal information.

## 9. International Data Transfers

Because Company of Profits serves founders in the United States and across Latin America, and relies on technology providers whose servers may be located outside your country of residence, your personal information may be transferred internationally. We take reasonable steps to ensure such providers maintain appropriate safeguards consistent with applicable data protection standards.

## 10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your state or country of residence, you may have rights that include:

- The right to know what personal information we have collected, used, or disclosed about you.
- The right to access and obtain a copy of your personal information.
- The right to correct inaccurate personal information.
- The right to request deletion of your personal information, subject to legal exceptions.
- The right to opt out of the sale or "sharing" of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising (Company of Profits does not sell or share personal information for advertising purposes).
- The right to non-discrimination for exercising your privacy rights.

**For California residents:** these rights are provided consistent with the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA). Company of Profits does not sell personal information and does not use or disclose sensitive personal information for purposes beyond what is necessary to provide the services you request.

**For residents of other U.S. states** with comprehensive privacy laws (such as Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, or others), we honor equivalent rights to the extent they apply to our processing activities.

**For Colombian and Latin American residents**, your rights of access, update, correction, and deletion (*habeas data*) are honored consistent with Colombia's Law 1581 of 2012 and Decree 1377 of 2013, as described in our Spanish-language Política de Privacidad, which governs for users in that jurisdiction.

To exercise any of the rights described above, email us at [info@companyofprofits.com](mailto:info@companyofprofits.com) with:

- Your full name and contact information.
- The right you wish to exercise.

- Any information reasonably necessary to verify your identity.

We will respond to verifiable requests within the timeframe required by applicable law, and in any event within a reasonable time, generally not exceeding 45 days (extendable once by an additional 45 days when reasonably necessary, with notice to you).
